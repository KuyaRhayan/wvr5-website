ABOUT WVR5

The Fraternal Order of Eagles – Philippine Eagles, Inc. Western Visayas Region 5 is a brotherhood committed to promoting unity, leadership, camaraderie, humanitarian service, and community involvement.

Through our clubs and dedicated members, we continue to uphold the spirit of service and strengthen our commitment to helping communities and those in need.

WE SERVE WITH PRIDE, UNITY, AND BROTHERHOOD.
