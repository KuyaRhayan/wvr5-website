ABOUT WVR5

United in Brotherhood. Committed to Service. Guided by the principles of Deo et Patria.

Our Brotherhood, Our Commitment

The Fraternal Order of Eagles – Philippine Eagles, Inc., Western Visayas Region 5 is a brotherhood committed to promoting unity, leadership, camaraderie, humanitarian service, and community involvement.

Through our member clubs and dedicated Eagles, we continue to support programs and activities that serve our communities and promote the values of brotherhood and patriotism.
